# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, StreamLine risks losing its high-value, loyal subscribers to specialized streaming competitors offering superior content discovery and curation. Rising churn and stagnant engagement will weaken its competitive position, making its extensive content library an increasingly ineffective differentiator.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to search Google, browse Letterboxd and IMDb, watch YouTube reviews, or seek recommendations on Reddit to find quality films. Even after paying for StreamLine, they spend more time deciding what to watch than actually watching content, potentially turning to competing streaming platforms.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must arrest rising subscriber churn and protect StreamLine's competitive position by solving the content discovery friction that drives quality-conscious viewers toward specialized streaming competitors.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For quality-conscious cinema enthusiasts overwhelmed by excessive content choices, StreamLine Spotlight will provide a trusted, human-curated discovery experience that helps them find exceptional films with less effort, without sacrificing access to StreamLine's extensive library. We must act now because plateauing engagement, rising churn, and growing competition threaten our ability to retain high-value subscribers.
