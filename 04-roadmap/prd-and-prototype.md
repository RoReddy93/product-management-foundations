## Generate your Simplified PRD
- **One thing my PRD makes explicit that a vague brief would have missed:** My PRD explicitly defines what not to build. A vague brief to create a human-curated discovery experience could lead to unnecessary investments in AI personalization, editorial management systems, or advanced filtering. By specifying a static, human-curated collection within the existing homepage, measurable acceptance criteria, and clear scope exclusions, my PRD ensures the team focuses on validating the core hypothesis within a three-week sprint rather than overengineering an unproven solution.
